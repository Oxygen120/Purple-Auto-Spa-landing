FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How much time does a full restoration take?

A standard Signature Wash takes about 60-90 minutes. However, a Full Paint Restoration or Mirror Polishing can take anywhere from 4 to 8 hours depending on the condition of the vehicle's paint.

Will machine polishing remove all scratches?

We can typically remove 80-95% of swirl marks and light surface scratches. Deep scratches that have penetrated the clear coat cannot be removed by polishing alone but can be significantly improved in appearance.

Do you use borewell water for washing?

Absolutely not. We use a dedicated Soft-Water filtration system to ensure that no mineral deposits or "hard water spots" are left on your car's delicate paintwork.

Why is your wash more expensive than a local wash?

Local washes often use harsh detergents (like Nirma/Surf) and dirty cloths that cause "spider-web" scratches. We use pH-neutral imported shampoos, the 2-bucket wash method, and 1200 GSM microfiber towels to ensure zero damage.
